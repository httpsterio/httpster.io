## About
Heya! My name is Sami and I'm a 35-year old developer/designer/artist. I'm living in central Finland with my wife. Originally from Denmark, but I've lived in Finland for the past ~26 years.

I'm really into fusion jazz. Like, really really into it. I'm also an avid rhythm gamer and my game of choice has always been 4-panel dancing games _(e.g. DDR, ITG and now ITGmania)_. I used to compete at a national level and after a long hiatus, I'm slowly grinding my way back.

I've also spent quite a few hours playing different Tetris games like [Tetr.io](https://ch.tetr.io/u/alajarvela/league). Aaaand about 800 hours playing Sombra in Overwatch...

But yeah, that's me. In general I'm trying to be a friendly dude and I don't mind yapping about just about anything with anyone. So, feel free to approach me!

## Now
I've been building some custom modules for ITGmania to allow heart rate data to be shown in-game. It's been refreshing to build something for other people to use!

Aside from that, I'm still basking in the experience that was the Masayoshi Takanaka gig last month in London. I feel like my head is set totally differently.

In general, life is good.
